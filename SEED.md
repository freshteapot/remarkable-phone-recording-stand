# The seed

This project began with a simple request:

> We are going to print a custom stand for video recording via a mobile.
>
> I have a remarkable 2 tablet.
>
> I want to create a tower, stand, to place my iphone (mini 12) so it can record the screen.
>
> The phone would be placed in portrait mode.
>
> The tablet would sit natually in portrait mode.
>
> I am left handed, so perhaps we need to factor in where the supports are, to allow freedom of writing with the pen, for right or left handed people.

That seed led to a handed-neutral, modular recording tower developed through conversation, CAD iteration and physical fit tests. The imperfect wording is retained because this is the original brief, not a retrospective specification.

## Design principles that came from it

- Record the complete reMarkable 2 screen from directly overhead.
- Hold the phone in portrait orientation.
- Keep both writing edges of the tablet unobstructed.
- Work equally well for left- and right-handed writers.
- Fit within a 250 x 250 x 250 mm printer volume.
- Use printable, modular joints that can be tested and revised independently.
